Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 25, 2026

Pearls Water Corporation ("Pearls Water," "we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it, and the choices you have. By using our website, you agree to the practices described here.

This policy covers our public website only. It does not cover information you may share with us directly by phone, in person, or through social media platforms such as Facebook, which have their own privacy policies.

1. Who We Are

Pearls Water Corporation is a water treatment systems and equipment provider based at Pearl Street, Business District, Metro Manila, Philippines. For any privacy-related questions, you can reach us at pearlswater48@gmail.com.

2. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide to us. When you submit our "Business Orientation" / quote request form, we collect your first and last name, contact number, the product(s) or services you're inquiring about, your preferred schedule, and any message you include.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and content delivery services (and the fonts and icon libraries we load) may automatically log standard technical data such as your browser type, device type, and general access times. We do not currently use analytics or advertising cookies to track your behavior across our site or other websites.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and schedule the business orientation, consultation, or quote you requested;
  • To provide information about our products, packages, and services;
  • To improve our website and the services we offer; and
  • To comply with legal obligations, where applicable.

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. How Your Information Is Processed and Stored

When you submit our orientation/quote request form, your details are transmitted securely to a Google Sheets spreadsheet maintained by our team, using a Google Apps Script integration. This means Google acts as a data processor on our behalf, subject to Google's own security and privacy practices. We take reasonable steps to limit access to this information to authorized personnel who need it to follow up on your request.

5. Third-Party Services

Our website loads a small number of third-party resources needed to make the site work, including:

  • Google Fonts (for typography) and Google Apps Script/Sheets (to process orientation form submissions);
  • Publicly hosted icon and utility libraries used purely for design purposes;
  • Links to our official social media pages (e.g. Facebook), which are governed by that platform's own privacy policy once you leave our site.

These services may process limited technical data (such as IP address) as part of normal internet functioning, but we do not use them to build advertising profiles of visitors.

6. Data Retention

We retain the information you submit through our orientation/quote form for as long as reasonably necessary to respond to your inquiry, provide the requested service, and maintain business records, after which it may be archived or deleted.

7. Your Rights

If you are located in the Philippines, you have rights under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173), including the right to:

  • Be informed that your personal data will be, are being, or have been processed;
  • Access your personal data that we hold;
  • Request correction of inaccurate or outdated personal data;
  • Object to or withdraw consent for the processing of your data, subject to legal or contractual restrictions;
  • Request the deletion or blocking of your personal data, where applicable; and
  • File a complaint with the National Privacy Commission if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at pearlswater48@gmail.com.

8. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for individuals who are at least 18 years old or otherwise able to enter into a business inquiry on their own behalf. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect the information you share with us. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for legal reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will indicate when the policy was last revised. Continued use of our website after changes are posted constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
